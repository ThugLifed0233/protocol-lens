# Project evolution

Protocol Lens did not begin as software. It emerged from a long-running pattern of personal health and performance work:

## Source era: spreadsheet training systems

Early artifacts included:

- powerlifting programs;
- top-set and back-off calculations;
- RPE and volume formulas;
- recovery and stress inputs;
- deload and test-week structures;
- bodyweight and lifting graphs.

These artifacts are design references. Their numbers are not automatically treated as verified performance records.

## Source era: compound research

Later research expanded into:

- supplements and nootropics;
- compound aliases;
- providers and product comparisons;
- community discussion and risk flags;
- personal start/stop periods and subjective outcomes.

Community attention is not effectiveness evidence. Research data and personal intervention data remain different layers.

## Software era: Apple-first baseline

The software begins with the most consistent longitudinal source: Apple Health.

The first technical question is narrow:

> What trends and within-source relationships can be understood from Apple Health and Apple workout data alone?

This creates a clean baseline before adding compound periods, nutrition, training spreadsheets, or biomarker imports.

## Honest repository history

Git history begins when the software implementation begins. Earlier source artifacts can be documented with their real approximate periods and provenance, but the repository will not backdate commits to imply that code existed earlier.

Planned release milestones:

| Release | Scope |
|---|---|
| v0.1 | Apple Health import, local storage, report, correlations |
| v0.2 | Better workout detail and period comparisons |
| v0.3 | Supplement and nootropic intervals |
| v0.4 | Before/during/after and lagged analysis |
