experiment: strength workouts account for most recorded sessions and nearly all recorded training
time. Walking is the main secondary activity, with smaller periods of interval training, running,
cycling, functional strength work, and rowing.

This is an observation about the imported record—not proof that unrecorded weeks were inactive or
that every workout was captured. Counts describe consistency and volume, not programme quality,
exercise selection, sets, repetitions, load, or effort.
