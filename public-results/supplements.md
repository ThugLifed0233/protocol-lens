# Supplement and nootropic result index

Protocol Lens currently contains 14 public-safe research profiles. Each profile separates:

1. the expected role;
2. the evidence boundary;
3. the Apple or manual metrics that can actually test the expectation;
4. personal before/during/after results, only after a usage period is confirmed and reviewed.

The complete machine-readable catalog is in
[`supplements.json`](supplements.json).

## Reconstructed history status

| Status | Profiles |

| Use history present | Creatine; whey and protein supplements; melatonin; ashwagandha; vegetarian omega-3; L-arginine; probiotics; fibre and psyllium; caffeine |
| Usage period needs confirmation | L-theanine; magnesium; L-carnitine |
| Research profile only | L-citrulline; curcumin |

“Use history present” means the historical record supports having used the item. It does not mean
that exact start and end dates are known.

## Current publication result

**Evidence profiles published:** 14  
**Personal Apple-linked supplement result rows published:** 0

This is intentional. Exact comparison windows have not yet been confirmed well enough to publish
before/during/after associations. Protocol Lens does not infer dates from product discussions,
purchases, or vague phrases such as “current” and “later.”

The first result becomes eligible only when all of these are true:

- the usage dates are confirmed;
- the expected metric is declared;
- enough Apple observations overlap the before and during windows;
- major confounders are reviewed;
- the profile and the period are explicitly approved for publication.

| L-carnitine | Comparable-workout duration and heart-rate context | Soreness and perceived recovery |
| Probiotics | Activity disruption only | Strain-specific gut symptoms |
| Fibre and psyllium | Hydration/activity context only | Stool frequency and comfort |
| Caffeine | Sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, workout context | Dose, perceived focus and side effects |

Apple associations are descriptive. A metric being higher or lower during a period does not show
that a supplement caused the difference.
